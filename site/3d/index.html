What you're seeing

Clouds are drawn where relative humidity is high (above the threshold you set). They're a stand-in for cloud, not the model's cloud itself, and Open Data has only 12 pressure levels between 1000 and 100 hPa.

Warm core shows air warmer than the average across the box at the same height, the signature of a tropical cyclone.

Inflow (teal) traces 925 hPa winds spiralling in near the sea; outflow (amber) traces 200 hPa winds fanning out at the top. Each streak is a puff of air: the bright head is where it is now, the tail where it has been, and brighter means faster. A faint frame round the box marks each level. The sea is coloured by 10 m wind speed, with isobars every 4 hPa.

Heights are exaggerated (the slider says by how much): the real atmosphere here is about 16 km deep and the box about 3000 km wide.
